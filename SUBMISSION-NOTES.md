## Oral-defense practice

1. Arrival Arcade: Which variables would you add to the Arrival Arcade state if the battery could run out, and why?

2. Transit Junction: Explain why an admissible heuristic and correct handling of repeated states both matter to A* optimality.

3. Relay Gardens: Why is a hard interference constraint different from a signal-strength objective, and how would you search for both?

4. Contest Court: Use the default tree to identify one pruned leaf, then explain why expectimax can recommend another branch.

5. Reward Foundry: Explain how value iteration and Q-learning obtain their updates, and show why reward design can fail even when optimization works.

6. Signal Market: Explain why a reliable detector can still produce a low posterior when the event is rare. Which sensor-independence assumption would you verify?

7. Fog Tunnel: Carry out one prediction and observation update by hand. Explain the two conditional-independence assumptions of this HMM.

8. Forecast Bridge: Explain how you would prevent leakage, measure missed failures, and derive a decision threshold from two actions’ costs.

9. Learning Gates: Demonstrate one perceptron update and explain the difference between a representation limit and a failure to optimize.
